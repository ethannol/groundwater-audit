# Arsenic (dissolved, 01000), routine groundwater, San Bernardino Co. — early (2000–11) vs late (2012–23)

**Population.** Sample-Routine results in the Groundwater subdivision, µg/L (2997 results: 1805 early, 1192 late). Field replicates, reference material, undetermined activities and non-groundwater matrices were excluded.

**Non-detects.** Early: 196 "Not Detected" plus 3 "Detected Not Quantified"; late: 46 "Not Detected". I did not substitute 0, the limit or half the limit as the main method. Medians are interval bounds (non-detects set to 0 vs. to their reporting limit). Early median is 1.9–2.0 µg/L (reported 1.95) and late is 3.3 µg/L. The bounds are tight because nearly all censored values sit below the median. Reporting limits ranged 0.12–5 µg/L early and 0.09–20 µg/L late and changed over time. The share above 10 µg/L is identifiable in both periods: early 15.6 %, late 30.8–31.2 %. The only unresolved censored result is one 2012 non-detect with a 20 µg/L limit.

**Comparability.** The medians are comparable in the sense that censoring does not drive them. Censoring fell from about 11 % to 4 % as limits dropped, but the limits are well below the medians. They are not comparable as a measure of change at the same wells. Only 295 of about 650 wells appear in both periods. Wells were sampled several times, so results are not independent. Methods and laboratories differ between periods.

**What it shows.** Pooled results from late sampling are higher than from early sampling (median about 1.95 → 3.3 µg/L; share >10 µg/L about 16 % → 31 %). On the 295 shared wells the difference is smaller but in the same direction (median 2.2 → 2.7; share >10 µg/L 18 % → 29 %).

**What it does not show.** It does not show that arsenic concentrations rose in any given well. Changes in which wells were sampled, and when, could produce the pooled difference. I computed no well-level uncertainty or trend test, so the comparison is descriptive.
