# Basin Z nitrate memo (synthetic data)

**Recommendation: decline not supported.** The data cannot show that nitrate declined at Basin Z wells.

**Evidence.** Thirty wells (C00–C29) have both 2005 and 2018 results. Compared well by well, 0 declined, 0 increased and 30 are indeterminate:
- 21 wells had non-detects in 2005 with a reporting limit of 2 mg/L as N. Their 2018 values (0.6–1.8) are all below 2, so they are consistent with no change and no decline can be shown.
- 9 wells have the same value in both years (2.04–3.26 mg/L), which is no change.

The early cohort median is censored (<2 mg/L). Substituting half the reporting limit gives 1.0, which is a convention and not a measurement. The late cohort median is 1.435 mg/L. If anything, that points to higher values, not a decline.

**Why it looks lower.** Thirty additional wells (N00–N29) were sampled only in 2018, with a median of about 0.5 mg/L. Pooling them with the cohort brings the 2018 median down to 0.91 mg/L. That compares different sets of wells, so it does not show a decline.

**Data issues constraining the conclusion**
- The reporting limit changed from 2 to 0.2 mg/L, so early non-detects cannot be compared with late detections (21 rows).
- Eight late results (C00–C07) came from "Synthetic Lab B (units not reported)", and the unit is blank (8 rows).
- The 30 N wells have no baseline (30 rows).
- The station table has a duplicate row for C03 (1 row).
- Nine late values are identical to the early values to three digits, which may mean copied or carried-forward data. This is unverified (9 rows).

**To resolve:** re-analyse archived 2005 samples at a lower limit, or collect new paired samples, and confirm Lab B's units and the identical values.
