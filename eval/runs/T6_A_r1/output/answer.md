# Basin Z nitrate: can we report a decline?

**Recommendation: cannot be determined. A decline is not supported by these data.**

## Evidence
- 30 wells (C00–C29) have results in both 2005 and 2018. Of these, 0 show a demonstrable decline, 0 an increase, and 30 are indeterminate.
- 21 wells were non-detect in 2005 at a reporting limit of 2 mg/L as N. Their 2018 values (0.47–1.8) are all below 2, so they are consistent with no change. The 2018 reporting limit (0.2) is ten times lower, so the two periods are not comparable for these wells.
- The other 9 wells have identical values in 2005 and 2018 (no change).
- Pooled cohort medians: early about 1.0 (non-detects set to half the limit; truly only known to be <2) and late 1.435. The early figure is a convention-dependent placeholder. With non-detects set to the limit it is 2.0. The "lower in 2018" impression is not a measured decline.
- Taking all 2018 rows (60 wells, median 0.91) and comparing them with 2005 is misleading. It adds 30 wells (N00–N29) sampled only in 2018, which have low values, so the network changed.

## Data issues
- Different reporting limits (2 vs 0.2) between the two periods: 21 rows.
- 30 wells sampled only in 2018: 30 rows.
- 8 late results from Synthetic Lab B have no units reported, and the lab differs from 2005.
- A duplicate station record for SYN-C03.
- 9 wells have exactly identical early and late values, which could be copied data and should be checked with the lab.
- Non-detects carry no numeric value.

## Next steps
Confirm the Lab B units and the identical values. Re-analyze with censored-data methods, or resample the 21 wells with a low reporting limit.
