# Dissolved arsenic (01000), San Bernardino County wells: 2000–2011 vs 2012–2023

**Data used.** Routine, groundwater-subdivision, dissolved-arsenic results (code 01000, µg/L). I excluded field replicates, "Not determined" activities, elutriation/soil-moisture/interstitial/leachate rows and total arsenic (01002). The cohort is the 295 wells with a routine result in both periods, using each well's last result per period. Non-detects were treated as intervals [0, reporting limit]. A pair is determinate only if the two intervals do not overlap.

**Findings.** 146 wells declined, 105 increased and 44 are indeterminate (30 of them involve a non-detect). Among the 251 determinate wells, 58.2% declined (exact 95% CI 51.8–64.3%; two-sided sign test p ≈ 0.011). Among all 295 cohort wells the declining share is 49.5%. The median late/early ratio over the 257 wells with detections in both periods is 0.96, so the typical change is small.

The data support a slight excess of declining over increasing wells. They do not support a claim of a large or general decline. Using only a 20% change as a meaningful difference, 212 of 295 wells are indeterminate, with 57 declining and 26 increasing. The direction holds there too, but it is a small-magnitude shift.

**Precision.** One decimal place on the share is about as much as is justified. The interval is for the share of determinate wells, not for the county.

**Assumptions the uncertainty depends on**
- Wells are independent. Neighbouring wells share aquifers and sampling programs, so the CI and p-value are probably somewhat too narrow.
- The cohort is wells that happened to be resampled, not a random sample of the county, so inference is to these wells only.
- Any difference between detected values counts as a direction. Analytical error is ignored; the 20% tolerance variant above shows how much this matters.
- The last result stands for the period. Multiple same-day samples exist, and the gap between paired results varies (early last-dates mostly 2009–2011, late 2012–2023).
- Methods, laboratories and reporting limits (0.09–20 µg/L) differ across time, and many results are Preliminary. Elevated limits (e.g. 20 µg/L in 2012) make some pairs indeterminate, not directional.
- Direction under zero, half-limit and full-limit substitution (a labelled sensitivity check only) is unchanged: declines exceed increases in every case (zero 149/108, half 153/107, full 152/108).
